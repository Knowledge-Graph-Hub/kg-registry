# GeoALS

## Overview

GeoALS is a nonprofit organization, founded in October 2024 by Pat Dolan, that uses geographic information systems (GIS) to improve care for people with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS), to accelerate ALS research, and to support advocacy. Its main platform, the ALS Geospatial Hub, runs on an ArcGIS platform donated through the Esri Nonprofit program and gives advocates and researchers access to maps and apps built for the ALS community.

## Data Content

- **ALS Clinic Locator**: a map of ALS clinics in the United States, described by GeoALS as the most complete ALS clinic database in the country. New clinics can be suggested through an online form.
- **Advisor dashboard**: reviews of ALS clinics contributed by people with ALS and their caregivers through the Ed Mytych Advisor Survey.
- **Legislative Viewer**: a map of legislators, with their photos, to support ALS advocacy.
- **Map and story galleries**: further maps and narrative map stories hosted on the ALS Geospatial Hub.

## Use Cases

- Helping people with ALS and their caregivers find and compare ALS clinics.
- Examining the geographic distribution of, and access to, ALS care.
- Connecting ALS advocates with their legislators.
