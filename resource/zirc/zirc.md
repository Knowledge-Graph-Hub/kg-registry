# Zebrafish International Resource Center (ZIRC)

The Zebrafish International Resource Center is a central repository for wild-type,
mutant and transgenic strains of zebrafish (*Danio rerio*). Lines are maintained as
live stocks or cryopreserved sperm and distributed as live adults or embryos. ZIRC
also stores and distributes antibodies, ESTs/cDNAs and gene probes, and provides
health services including diagnostic pathology for zebrafish and other small
laboratory fish (RRID:SCR_005065).

The online catalog listed 44,483 zebrafish lines on 2026-10-03.

## Relationship to ZFIN

ZIRC is a sister organization to the Zebrafish Information Network (ZFIN), also at
the University of Oregon. Catalog entries link to the corresponding ZFIN records, and
ZFIN allele and feature pages list ZIRC as the current source when a line is
available there.

## Data Access

- Fish lines: https://zebrafish.org/fish/lineAll.php (search by genotype, allele,
  construct, affected gene, line type, phenotype, availability and ZIRC catalog ID)
- Antibodies: https://zebrafish.org/abs/absAll.php
- ESTs/cDNAs: https://zebrafish.org/est/estAll.php

No bulk download or API is offered. ZFIN's downloads do not include a ZIRC-specific
file.

## Funding

ZIRC is funded by NIH Office of Research Infrastructure Programs grant P40OD011021,
in collaboration with the Eunice Kennedy Shriver National Institute of Child Health
and Human Development (NICHD). Zoltán M. Varga is the contact PI.
