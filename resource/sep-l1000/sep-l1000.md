# SEP-L1000

SEP-L1000 is a web portal from the Ma'ayan Laboratory at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai that prioritizes adverse drug reactions (ADRs) for approved drugs and preclinical small molecules. It was built with support from the BD2K-LINCS Data Coordination and Integration Center and the NIH Illuminating the Druggable Genome program, and accompanies Wang, Clark and Ma'ayan 2016 (Bioinformatics).

## Data Content

Known on-label ADRs come from SIDER and off-label ADRs from OFFSIDES (processed FAERS reports), with ADR terms mapped to MedDRA preferred terms. Features for each compound include characteristic direction signatures from the strongest LINCS L1000 experiment, Gene Ontology enrichment vectors derived from those signatures, MACCS and PubChem chemical fingerprints, and cell morphology profiles from the Broad Institute MLPCN profiling dataset. DrugBank names and synonyms were used to map marketed drugs to L1000 compounds. Extremely randomized trees classifiers, one per ADR, were applied to all profiled compounds; the portal reports predictions for 20,412 compounds and 1,054 side effects.

## Access

The portal lets users browse drugs and side effects, search, and view side effect-GO term and side effect-phenotype networks. The download page provides the processed side effect matrices, signature and fingerprint matrices, morphology profiles, and metadata as CSV files. The site is served over HTTP only; the HTTPS address does not respond. It was online and its database backend was answering queries on 2026-10-19.
