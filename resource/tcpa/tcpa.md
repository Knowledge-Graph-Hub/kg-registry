# The Cancer Proteome Atlas (TCPA)

## Overview

The Cancer Proteome Atlas (TCPA) is a portal for cancer functional proteomics data generated by reverse phase protein arrays (RPPA) at MD Anderson Cancer Center. It covers protein expression in patient tumors from The Cancer Genome Atlas and in cancer cell lines, including the Cancer Cell Line Encyclopedia. The current RPPA500 compendium covers about 9,000 samples measured with 447 protein markers, 90 of them post-translationally modified (for example, phosphorylated) forms.

## Access

- Patient Cohorts: RPPA500 protein expression for TCGA tumors.
- Cancer Cell Lines: RPPA data for cancer cell lines (the MD Anderson Cell Lines Project, MCLP).
- Cancer Perturbed Proteomics Atlas (CPPA): proteomic responses of cell lines to perturbations.

The portal (tcpaportal.org) now redirects to tcpa.drbioright.org. TCPA data also underpin the DrBioRight natural-language analytics platform. Use is free for academic non-profit institutions; other users need a commercial license.
