  for genomic and other omics data. Its core archives include the Genome Sequence
  Archive (GSA) for raw sequence reads and its controlled-access counterpart GSA-Human,
  the Genome Warehouse (GWH) for genome assemblies and annotations, the Genome Variation
  Map (GVM), and BioProject and BioSample databases for study and sample metadata.
  NGDC also hosts many curated knowledgebases (such as LncBook) and Database Commons,
  a catalog of biological databases worldwide. Resources are described in an annual
  Nucleic Acids Research database issue paper.

# National Genomics Data Center

The National Genomics Data Center (NGDC) at the China National Center for Bioinformation (CNCB) archives and curates genomic and other omics data. Its core archives are the Genome Sequence Archive (GSA and GSA-Human), Genome Warehouse (GWH), Genome Variation Map (GVM), BioProject and BioSample. It also hosts dozens of curated knowledgebases and Database Commons, a catalog of biological databases.

NGDC was formerly known as the BIG Data Center (BIGD) at the Beijing Institute of Genomics. Bulk data are available from https://download.cncb.ac.cn/. The NGDC website policies include disclaimers and ethics terms but state no data license.
