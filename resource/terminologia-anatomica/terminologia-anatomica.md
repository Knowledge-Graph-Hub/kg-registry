# Terminologia Anatomica (TA2)

## Overview

Terminologia Anatomica is the international standard for human anatomical terminology, maintained by FIPAT for the IFAA. It gives official Latin terms, English equivalents, and TA identifiers for anatomical structures. The second edition (TA2) was published in 2019 and adopted in 2020, replacing the 1998 first edition (TA98). The Foundational Model of Anatomy takes Latin terms and synonyms from Terminologia Anatomica and carries TA identifiers as cross-references.

## Access

- TA2Viewer: open web browser for TA2 terms, built by the IFAA and the Open Anatomy Project at Brigham and Women's Hospital.
- The official FIPAT TA2 publication, released as front matter plus numbered PDF parts hosted by Dalhousie University Libraries.

The TA2 publication is under CC BY-ND 4.0; unaltered PDFs may be freely copied and distributed, and derivative works need permission from the FIPAT chair. Individual terms are treated as public domain.
