# MicroMap (Graphomics Knowledge Graph Platform)
**Authors:** Khandavalli, Varun
**Journal:** Zenodo (2026)
**DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.22800749](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22800749)

## Content

A microbiome knowledge graph platform powered by Neo4j, with a reviewable ingestion pipeline (MapForge) that turns raw tabular and BioCypher-adapter-sourced data into parameterized Cypher bundles a reviewer can approve before any node lands in the graph. Not affiliated with the identically-named MicroMap microbiome-metabolism visualization resource (Thiele lab, npj Biofilms and Microbiomes, 2025, https://dataverse.harvard.edu/dataverse/micromap).
