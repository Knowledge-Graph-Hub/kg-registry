The Gene Transcription Regulation Database (GTRD; http://gtrd.biouml.org/) 
contains uniformly annotated and processed NGS data related to gene 
transcription regulation: ChIP-seq, ChIP-exo, DNase-seq, MNase-seq, ATAC-seq and 
RNA-seq. With the latest release, the database has reached a new level of data 
integration. All cell types (cell lines and tissues) presented in the GTRD were 
arranged into a dictionary and linked with different ontologies (BRENDA, Cell 
Ontology, Uberon, Cellosaurus and Experimental Factor Ontology) and with related 
experiments in specialized databases on transcription regulation (FANTOM5, 
ENCODE and GTEx). The updated version of the GTRD provides an integrated view of 
transcription regulation through a dedicated web interface with advanced 
browsing and search capabilities, an integrated genome browser, and table 
reports by cell types, transcription factors, and genes of interest.
